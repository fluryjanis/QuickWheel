What Is QuickWheel?

QuickWheel is a browser extension that uses a radial navigation wheel and mouse gestures to place frequent browser actions directly around your pointer.

Instead of shifting your cursor across high-resolution displays to click small tabs, buttons, or submenus, you execute actions through fast directional flicks.

Why a Radial Navigation Wheel?

Standard browser controls require pixel-precise movement to distant screen edges. In contrast, a radial menu positions every target at an identical, comfortable distance around your cursor.

Because selections rely on direction rather than coordinates, navigation quickly converts into instinctive muscle memory.

Workflow

Hold → Flick → Action

Effortless browser navigation in three simple steps.

1

Hold

Press and hold middle-click or press Alt + Q.

2

Flick

The radial navigation wheel opens centered on your cursor. Flick toward an action.

3

Done

Release the button to instantly execute the action.

Privacy-First Engineering

QuickWheel operates entirely locally on your machine. It has zero external tracking, no telemetry, and does not record or transmit browsing data.
