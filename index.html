What Is QuickWheel?

QuickWheel is an ultra-fast, lightweight browser extension engineered around a single-ring radial navigation wheel and intuitive mouse gestures.

Instead of hunting for buttons across sprawling browser tabs, toolbars, and nested context menus, QuickWheel draws essential browser actions directly around your mouse cursor the moment you need them.

  • Hold middle mouse button or press Alt + Q
  • Flick toward one of the 8 surrounding directions
  • Release to execute actions with zero cognitive friction

Why a Radial Navigation Wheel?

Fitts's Law dictates that moving your cursor to a small tab icon or menu bar at the edge of an ultra-wide display takes time and breaks concentration.

A radial menu arranges target actions symmetrically at an identical distance around your cursor. Because directional flicks rely on muscle memory and angular trajectory rather than pixel precision, triggering an action becomes as rapid as an involuntary reflex.

  • Zero travel distance: The menu appears precisely where your eyes are focused
  • Motor memory over visual scanning: Flick without looking
  • Preserves native middle-click autoscrolling whenever you don't flick

The 8 Core Browser Actions

Duplicate Tab

Focus Search

Mute / Unmute Tab

Copy Link

Copies the clean URL of your active browser tab directly into your clipboard ready to share.

North-West ↖

Reopen Tab

Accidentally closed research tabs? Resurrect the most recently closed tab with a quick northwest flick.

The QuickWheel Story

The project started as QuickWheel, and was temporarily evolved into Research Wheel to experiment with deep academic annotation and synthesis features across versions 2.0.0 and 2.0.1.

However, community feedback and strong Firefox usage signals confirmed how much people missed the lightning-fast, general-purpose browser utility. QuickWheel has now returned to its roots—sharper, faster, and carrying over all the gesture reliability improvements from Research Wheel's development cycle.

Privacy-First by Design

QuickWheel operates 100% locally on your computer. It does not contain telemetry, does not record URLs, does not use analytics, and never communicates with any remote servers. Your browsing habits remain strictly yours.
