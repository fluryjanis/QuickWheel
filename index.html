What is QuickWheel?

A radial navigation wheel for the browser.

QuickWheel is a lightweight browser extension that puts common browser actions directly around your cursor.

Instead of moving through browser menus or reaching for controls around the screen, you can hold middle-click, flick in a direction, and release.

Why a radial wheel?

Use direction instead of hunting for controls.

A radial menu gives each action a fixed direction around the pointer. Once you learn where an action lives, the gesture becomes a simple movement rather than another trip through a menu.

QuickWheel keeps that interaction inside a single ring so the interface stays compact and easy to scan.

Built Around the Gesture

Designed to stay out of the way

QuickWheel is built around a simple interaction instead of adding another permanent toolbar to your browser.

1

Single-ring design

Eight equal radial sectors keep the wheel compact and make every action part of the same visual system.

2

Gesture-first control

The interaction is based on the direction of your pointer rather than clicking tiny interface controls.

3

Middle-click aware

Quick middle-clicks below the activation threshold are left available for normal browser behavior.

4

Scroll protection

Scrolling is locked while the wheel is active so the page does not move underneath the gesture.

5

Page-style isolation

The wheel interface uses Shadow DOM isolation so page styles are less likely to interfere with its layout.

6

Lightweight by design

QuickWheel stays inactive until you invoke it instead of occupying permanent browser interface space.

Workflow

Hold → Flick → Release

The whole interaction is built around three simple steps.

1

Hold

Hold the middle mouse button anywhere on a webpage, or activate QuickWheel with Alt + Q.

2

Flick

Move the pointer away from the center toward the action you want to use.

3

Release

Let go to execute the selected browser action immediately.

Privacy

No analytics or external tracking

QuickWheel does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or telemetry to monitor how you browse. Its normal tab, search, navigation, and clipboard actions are handled by the extension itself.

Translation is the exception: when you use Translate Page, QuickWheel sends the readable text it is translating to Google Translate and uses the returned translations to replace that text on the page.
